## Acceptance matrix

| Journey | Passing result |
| --- | --- |
| Service signup and billing | Email verification, Stripe test checkout and saved card work. Interrupted checkout can resume. A direct call tests Cara but leaves collection on hold. A qualifying forwarded call activates one seven-day trial. A carrier that omits forwarding evidence reaches support. Duplicate callbacks, failed payment, setup cancellation and an existing customer preserve the correct entitlements and charges. |
| Answer and follow up | Real Irish phone line, correct tenant and disclosure, configured greeting, hours, offered and excluded services, after-hours answer, unknown question and requested callback. Check concurrent calls, a cold first call, interruptions and hang-up. Duration, both speakers, summary, outcome, recording where enabled, follow-up and notification agree with the actual call. |
| Owner workflow | Find the call on a phone and desktop. Resolve and reopen the follow-up under a named staff member, publish a previously unknown answer and call again to hear it. Retry a failed notification. Reconcile an ambiguous provider send before retrying. |
| Retail pilot | For a Donegal Town pilot, try a bakery collection enquiry, a deli question, a current Rewards price, an expired offer and a shelf-stock question. The order request reaches the correct department with caller details. Collection is represented as requested until staff confirm it. Transfer only where the store’s line and destination support it. |
| Roles and privacy | Use two businesses plus owner, staff and read-only roles. Invite and revoke access. Check call media, transcripts, knowledge, settings and tenant boundaries. Block a designated caller. Erase a test caller during post-processing and verify content stays erased. Use aged fixtures for the 30-day access and storage-cleanup checks. |
| Public site and operations | Play the sample on mobile, follow the contact and login links, verify a bookable slot and a booking fallback, and complete signup from the actual launch CTA. Verify support receipt, alerts, a successful scheduler HTTP response, failed-job visibility and the operator’s rollback procedure. |
